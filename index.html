Studio

Griho Naksha works from a simple premise: a building should feel like it was always meant for its site. We spend the first weeks of every project outdoors — reading the light, the wind, the neighbors — before a single wall goes on paper.

The practice is small by choice. Every drawing that leaves the office has been through the founding architect's hands, and every client works directly with the person building their project, from first sketch to final handover.

How a project moves

Three stages, in order, from first site visit to the last coat of paint.

01 — Concept

Site & brief

Weeks of walking the site and listening to how you'll actually use the space, before any drawing begins.

02 — Development

Drawing & detail

Plans, sections, and material choices refined together with you, with a physical model at every major review.

03 — Execution

Site supervision

Weekly site visits through construction, so what gets built matches what was drawn — and improves on it where it can.
